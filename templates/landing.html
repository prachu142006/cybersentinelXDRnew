🛡 Cyber Security Best Practices

Cyber attacks often succeed because of simple mistakes. Follow these best practices to stay secure.

Use Strong Passwords

Create passwords with at least 12 characters using uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.

Enable Multi-Factor Authentication

Always enable MFA to add an extra layer of protection against unauthorized access.

Beware of Phishing Emails

Avoid clicking unknown links or downloading attachments from suspicious emails.

Keep Software Updated

Install security updates regularly to protect against newly discovered vulnerabilities.

Verify Website URLs

Always check HTTPS and verify the website domain before entering sensitive information.

Avoid Unsecured Networks

Do not access important accounts over public Wi-Fi unless you are using a trusted VPN.
